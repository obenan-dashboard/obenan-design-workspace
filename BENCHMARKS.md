# Benchmarks

## Workspace completeness

- The five current Markdown design sources are present and readable.
- The five design sources, original brand books, and three old-logo references
  expected in the starter are present.
- The quick-start logo and brand-book files are real files, not symlinks or
  cloud placeholders.
- `FILE_INDEX.tsv` and `MANIFEST.sha256` cover the delivered workspace.
- A fresh extraction preserves the starter filenames, file counts, sizes, and
  hashes.

## Agent orientation

An agent succeeds when it can identify:

- the mandatory source order
- current versus legacy identity
- the five design principles
- the original color and typography rules
- the restricted status of the legacy logo
- the applicable imagery, accessibility, motion, locale, and product-truth
  gates
- the fact that this workspace is not release authority

## Design quality

- One visual purpose per section and one dominant action.
- Meaningful material and motion; no ornamental AI chrome.
- Product UI remains neutral-first and evidence-led.
- Approved assets and components are reused before new variants are admitted.
- Customer-facing work is responsive, keyboard-accessible, contrast-safe,
  locale-safe, reduced-motion-safe, and truthful.

## Evidence of done

For design work, evidence includes rendered mobile and desktop output, exact
asset paths, accessibility and interaction checks, and an independent review
against the accepted design decision.

For implementation or release, use the production repository's own spec,
tests, CI, preview, and release gates. This reference workspace alone cannot
prove implementation or live state.
