## Publication rules

1. Do not copy the whole Drive library into the public repository.
2. Select the exact file only after its intended story, route and crop are
   approved.
3. Preserve the original separately; create a web derivative with an explicit
   filename, dimensions, crop record and SHA-256.
4. Use responsive `sizes`, modern formats where appropriate and a quality cap
   that preserves faces and food without bloating LCP.
5. Alt text should describe the useful visual information in the route context.
   Do not insert names, endorsements or performance claims that the source does
   not establish.
6. Verify focal point and text collision at desktop, tablet and mobile sizes.
7. Do not use a hospitality image to imply that every Obenan customer or route
   belongs to hospitality.
