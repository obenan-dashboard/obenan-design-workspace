# Messaging reference lane

This directory preserves source material used for Obenan messaging research.
Files here are evidence inputs, not approved customer-facing copy.

## Source states

- `sources/`: immutable source snapshots with recorded provenance.
- Research critique belongs under `research/messaging/`, never beside a source.
- A source file may be outdated, incomplete, or internally inconsistent. Its
  presence here is not approval of every claim it contains.

## Authority

Only files under `knowledge/messaging/approved/` may become reusable messaging
authority after Seven's explicit approval. Research and proposed synthesis must
remain clearly labelled as non-canonical.
