# Agent Rules

This file governs every agent working from this design workspace.

## Before starting

1. Read `START_HERE.md` completely.
2. Read this file completely.
3. Read `STATUS.md` completely.
4. Follow the task-specific source order in `START_HERE.md`.
5. Inspect the actual quick-start assets instead of guessing from filenames.
   Use the paired FULL archive when deeper source or motion assets are needed.

## Design contract

- Material must carry meaning. Do not add decorative glass, ambient gradients,
  ornamental effects, or generic AI chrome.
- Data and proof are the material. Prefer evidence, ledgers, grids, citations,
  and honest states over decorative dashboard cards.
- Preserve Obenan identity. Do not redraw, regenerate, stretch, approximate, or
  recolor the logo.
- Keep product UI restrained: greyscale carries the interface; accent color has
  a specific job.
- Protect glance economy: one visual purpose per section, one dominant action,
  short copy, strong hierarchy, and details on demand.
- Reuse approved components and assets before creating variants.
- Human first, product proven. The merchant is the hero; Obenan is the guide.
- Motion must clarify state or causality, respect reduced motion, and never hide
  essential content.
- Preserve accessibility, responsive behavior, locale safety, performance, and
  product truth.

## Legacy identity rule

The rainbow wave and lowercase wordmark are included for identity history and
source accuracy. Do not reintroduce them into a current page, social preview,
application surface, or campaign unless the assigned task contains an explicit
human design decision authorizing that use.

Legacy does not mean disposable. Never alter or regenerate these files.

## Customer-language and claim rules

- Lead with the customer's fear, workload, and relief.
- Avoid `AI-powered` positioning and old Local SEO positioning.
- Do not call Obenan a platform, dashboard, software, or tool when direct
  customer language is available.
- Do not claim guaranteed AI discovery, indexing, recommendation, training,
  feeding, or security.
- Publishing truth: Facebook and Instagram only.
- Google truth: listings and business-data distribution, not social publishing.
- AI discovery is a downstream possibility, never a guarantee.

## Execution preflight

Before implementation, return:

1. canonical design sources read
2. what is current, legacy, exploratory, and unresolved
3. customer problem and intended outcome
4. assets and components to reuse
5. exact production repo and file ceiling
6. acceptance criteria and test-first plan
7. responsive, accessibility, locale, reduced-motion, and performance plan
8. claim and product-truth checks
9. unresolved human decisions
10. authorized release rung

Do not implement while a design decision, source owner, customer claim, asset
approval, or path boundary is unresolved.

## Coordination and proof

- Files are memory; chat is not. Update `STATUS.md` when shared state changes.
- Log durable decisions in `DECISIONS_LOG.md`.
- Claims are not proof. Verify rendered output and behavior before calling work
  complete.
- Do not assume you are the only agent. Stop on a second writer or overlapping
  mutable surface.
- Distinguish local, committed, PR, preview, merged, production-deployed, and
  live.
- Do not push, merge, deploy, alias, publish, contact people, or clean up unless
  the task explicitly authorizes that exact action.
