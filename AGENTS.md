# Agent Rules

This file governs every agent working from this design workspace. It is
Layer 1 of the routing hierarchy in `START_HERE.md`.

## Before starting

1. Read `START_HERE.md`, this file, `STATUS.md`, and `knowledge/README.md`
   (Claude Code also reads `CLAUDE.md`).
2. Retrieve only your task-type row from `knowledge/TASK_ROUTER.md` and read
   only that bundle. Do not preload the router or the evidence library.
3. Open work by stating, in this order: task type; surface family; requested
   outcome; authority granted (repository, paths, release rung, external
   actions); selected reading bundle; known unresolved gates.
4. Inspect the actual quick-start assets instead of guessing from filenames.
   Use the paired FULL archive when deeper source or motion assets are needed.

## Design contract

- Material must carry meaning: no decorative glass, ambient gradients,
  ornamental effects, or generic AI chrome.
- Data and proof are the material: evidence, ledgers, citations, and honest
  states over decorative cards.
- Preserve Obenan identity. Never redraw, regenerate, stretch, approximate, or
  recolor the logo; reuse approved assets and components before new variants.
- Product UI stays restrained: greyscale carries, accent has a specific job,
  one visual purpose per section, one dominant action, details on demand.
- Human first, product proven. The merchant is the hero; Obenan is the guide.
- Motion clarifies state or causality, respects reduced motion, and never hides
  essential content.
- Preserve accessibility, responsive behavior, locale safety, performance, and
  product truth.

The proposed Design Core expands this into principles, constants, and a
surface matrix (proposed, not approved).

## Legacy identity rule

The rainbow wave and lowercase wordmark are kept for identity history and
source accuracy. Do not reintroduce them into a current page, social preview,
application surface, or campaign unless the task carries an explicit human
design decision authorizing that use (status: DR-D01 in
`knowledge/design/proposed/DECISION_REQUESTS_v1.md`; bytes source-verified,
authority unresolved). Legacy does not mean disposable: never alter or
regenerate these files.

## Customer-language and claim rules

- Lead with the customer's fear, workload, and relief.
- Avoid `AI-powered` positioning and old Local SEO positioning.
- Do not call Obenan a platform, dashboard, software, or tool when direct
  customer language is available.
- Do not claim guaranteed AI discovery, indexing, recommendation, training,
  feeding, or security.
- Publishing truth: Facebook and Instagram only.
- Google truth: listings and business-data distribution, not social publishing.
- AI discovery is a downstream possibility, never a guarantee.

The proposed Messaging Core holds the claim-state ladder, verb gates, and
language families (proposed, not approved). Both cores are located through
`knowledge/README.md`.

## Retrieval rules

- Read only the routed operating bundle before beginning.
- Retrieve deep research through `research/README.md` when: a claim requires
  provenance; sources disagree; the task introduces a new surface or pattern;
  a decision is unresolved; cultural, behavioral, or customer-language nuance
  matters; or the task is research, strategy, or adversarial review.
- Never exclude deep research from a report when it materially supports,
  contradicts, or limits a conclusion.
- Cite or link research rather than copying it into operating documents. Every
  rule stays traceable to evidence without forcing anyone to load it all.
- Absence of optional evidence produces PARTIAL with a recorded limitation; an
  optional evidence gap never halts bounded work.

## Execution preflight

Before implementation, return:

1. the opening statement from "Before starting" item 3
2. canonical design sources read, and what is current, legacy, exploratory,
   or unresolved
3. customer problem and intended outcome
4. assets and components to reuse
5. exact production repo, branch, and file ceiling
6. acceptance criteria and test-first plan
7. claim and product-truth checks
8. unresolved human decisions and approval gates
9. authorized release rung
10. the ten-item design preflight in
    `knowledge/design/proposed/DESIGN_CORE_v1.md` section 8, each item marked
    PASS, PARTIAL, or STOP

PASS means the item is proven. PARTIAL means bounded work may proceed with the
limitation recorded; an optional evidence gap is always PARTIAL, never a stop.
STOP means a hard-stop condition below applies.

## Hard-stop conditions (authoritative)

Stop and do not implement while any of these is unresolved:

1. release, repository, or path authority for the change is missing or
   exceeded;
2. the identity asset is not the approved one, or would be drawn, retyped,
   recolored, regenerated, or approximated;
3. a customer-facing claim, number, quote, or image lacks provenance;
4. a safety, security, privacy, or accessibility defect would harm or exclude
   a user;
5. a locked surface would change without its approval record;
6. essential product truth would be misrepresented (claim state, custody, or
   what Obenan does and does not do);
7. a second writer holds the same mutable surface.

Within customer-visible design work, apply the hard-stop conditions defined in
this AGENTS.md together with all higher-level user, safety, authorization and
repository rules. DESIGN_CORE section 8 supplies a proposed design-quality
checklist until approved; it does not override higher authority.

## Coordination and proof

- Files are memory; chat is not. Update `STATUS.md` when shared state changes.
- Log durable decisions in `DECISIONS_LOG.md`.
- Claims are not proof. Verify rendered output and behavior before calling work
  complete.
- Do not assume you are the only agent (hard stop 7).
- Distinguish local, committed, PR, preview, merged, production-deployed, and
  live. Verified code at an inspected Git reference is authoritative for
  implementation behavior, compatibility, routes, and dependencies at that
  reference; it is not approval of design intent, brand direction,
  deployment, or live state.
- Do not push, merge, deploy, alias, publish, contact people, or clean up
  unless the task authorizes that exact action, and do not mark anything
  approved or canonical without Seven's recorded decision.
