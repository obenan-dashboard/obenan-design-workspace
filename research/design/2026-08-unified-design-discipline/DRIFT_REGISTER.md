# Drift register

Date: 2026-08-19

State: `OBSERVED_DRIFT / PROPOSED_DIRECTION / NOT_APPROVAL`

A drift item is a place where two sources that should agree do not, or where
one source contradicts its own stated rule. Each item records the evidence,
the intended behavior where it could be found, the likely owner, the
compatibility constraint that makes a fix non-trivial, and a proposed
direction. Severity: `S1` blocks a unified system, `S2` erodes it, `S3`
cosmetic or hygiene. Counts and paths are from `EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md`.

## Drift that is not a defect (recorded so nobody "fixes" it)

- The website and the product have different densities and different
  emotional jobs; that is intended (`DESIGN_PRINCIPLES` P8, BRAND_EMOTION
  principle 5, this run's mood matrix).
- The locked public chrome labels and routes are frozen by protocol; any
  change is a redesign requiring founder approval.
- Customer-branded executive briefings may carry customer colors by design;
  the drift is only that they are expressed as page CSS rather than through
  the whitelabel seam.
- Alpha-black greys (`#00000066` and friends) are equivalent to the brand
  book's solid greys on white and are a legitimate technique on white; they
  only become drift on tinted or dark canvases.
