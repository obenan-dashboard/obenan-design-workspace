## Scope and method

This report audits the February dataset claim by claim for source existence, quotation integrity, currency, evidentiary strength, and applicability to Obenan. It does not approve copy, define a brand system, or replace Product, Legal, privacy, or founder review.

The audit separates four layers:

1. **Observed evidence:** what the February file, current governed workspace, live first-party pages, and cited research actually say.
2. **Dataset claim:** what the February file asserts, including its source attribution.
3. **Interpretation:** what can reasonably be inferred within the evidence limits.
4. **Recommendation:** what may survive into synthesis, what needs correction, and what must be held.

Statuses have fixed meanings:

- **Supported:** the strongest located source says materially the same thing within the same scope.
- **Supported with qualification:** the core is traceable, but the dataset broadens, absolutizes, or changes its application.
- **Outdated:** current first-party evidence has superseded the number, capability, or state.
- **Misattributed:** the attributed source supports only a narrower or different statement.
- **Overextended:** a real source or effect is applied beyond its studied population, outcome, or context.
- **Unsupported:** the claim is testable but no adequate evidence supports it, or stronger evidence contradicts the universal form.
- **Unverifiable:** no authoritative primary trace was located by the cutoff. This is not proof that the words were never spoken.

## Executive verdict

The February dataset contains a useful drafting skeleton, but it is not a reliable communication operating system. Its strongest survivable elements are customer-centered framing, guide-like empathy, clear problem-to-outcome structure, specific proof, a simple next step, honest scarcity, and attention to detail. These are methods or editorial preferences, not deterministic laws.

The dataset repeatedly converts vendor teaching into neuroscience, treats contextual behavioral effects as universal rules, presents unsupported ratios and timings as precision, and attributes several quotations without a primary trace. Its most serious risk is not academic imprecision. It is the movement from reassurance into fear, shame, status pressure, mind-reading, and product overclaim.

Current Obenan first-party evidence also supersedes material February product claims. The live public roster states 104 integrations, 100 currently updating, 18 reply-capable surfaces, and two post-capable surfaces. Current product language describes connected platforms, capability variation, governed approvals, prepared actions, and optional configured automation. That evidence does not support 130 directories, universal review handling, zero manual work, every language, self-managing software, or guaranteed AI recommendation.

The February file should remain quarantined as historical evidence. Retain only the bounded items listed below, verify all product statements at release time, and require Seven's approval before any wording enters brand canon or external use.

## What the February dataset gets right

The following findings are directly observed or adequately traceable, with limits:

- StoryBrand does teach a seven-part framework in which the customer is the character and the brand acts as a guide with empathy and authority [S07-S10]. This is vendor doctrine, not proof that every message must use SB7.
- StoryBrand currently teaches PEACE as Problem, Empathy, Answer, Change, and End Result [S12]. The structure exists. Claims that it bypasses analysis or attaches to survival instincts are not supported.
- Narrative structure can improve comprehension and recall, and narrative messages can have small persuasive advantages in some studied contexts [S22-S24]. This does not validate SB7 specifically or guarantee conversion.
- People use effort-reducing heuristics [S27]. This supports clarity and hierarchy, but not the claim that analytical effort triggers a universal exit response.
- Emotion matters in decisions, including B2B decisions, alongside functional, symbolic, relational, intellectual, and cost considerations [S34-S35]. Emotion is not the sole or actual driver of every purchase.
- Price can influence perceived quality for consumer products, and real scarcity cues can influence purchase intentions [S32-S33]. Effects vary by design, product, consumer, and scarcity type. Neither finding supports never discounting or manufacturing exclusivity.
- Luxury Academy directly uses the phrase "Communicate, Relate, Connect" and promotes empathy, trust, rapport, consultative discovery, and bespoke recommendations [S13-S16]. It does not establish the February file's rigid sequential stages or absolute ban on options.
- The StoryBrand five-second homepage test is a real vendor diagnostic [S11]. It is a useful clarity check, not a validated conversion law or a mandatory page template.
- Current Obenan evidence supports governed location and business-truth work across connected surfaces, with capability varying by destination and field [S19-S21]. Current evidence also supports prepared actions, approval controls, dashboard workflows, and compatible assistant workflows.

## Obenan implications as practical rules

1. Start with an evidenced customer concern and the credible relief, not internal category machinery.
2. Sound like a capable peer. Explain, recommend, and accompany. Do not lecture, diagnose, or prescribe with false certainty.
3. Preserve agency. A direct next step can be clear without implying that waiting is foolish or that a competitor will win.
4. Use customer facts only when directly observed. Do not assume workload, skill, team structure, tools, emotional state, or purchase motive.
5. Treat StoryBrand and PEACE as drafting prompts. They do not prove conversion and must not override product truth, legal clarity, or customer evidence.
6. Use literal commercial and legal terms. Premium tone is not permission to relabel price, fee, contract, customer, limitation, or approval.
7. Attach every product statement to the supported surface, field, permission, automation rule, approval state, and verification evidence.
8. Do not promise AI indexing, model training inclusion, recommendation, rank, revenue, bookings, or autonomous downstream execution.
9. Keep named platforms and mechanisms out of lead customer messaging unless the context genuinely requires a supported detail.
10. Refresh time-sensitive counts and capabilities at release time. A first-party page establishes the public claim state, not independent delivery proof.

## Evidence gaps and decision gates

### Evidence gaps

- No independent evidence links Obenan's messaging, product, or the combined framework to conversion, revenue, bookings, recommendation, or retention outcomes.
- No segment-specific first-party customer-language corpus was supplied to validate emotional states, vocabulary, or objections.
- Several attributed Donald Miller and Paul Russell quotations lack a primary trace.
- Current first-party product pages establish public claims, not independent production or destination-level delivery proof.
- No legal or privacy analysis was supplied for behavioral tracking, recipient profiling, scarcity claims, or automated outreach.
- No controlled Obenan tests validate the proposed ratios, timings, CTA rules, sound bites, or channel sequences.

### Decision gates

1. **Founder gate:** Seven must approve any retained messaging principle or wording before it becomes canon.
2. **Product gate:** A named Product owner must verify every capability, count, workflow, supported surface, field, permission, approval path, and downstream boundary at release time.
3. **Evidence gate:** Outcome language requires dated, scoped evidence and a distinction among observed, prepared, approved, delivered, and downstream result.
4. **Customer gate:** Audience pain, workload, motive, and vocabulary require direct first-party evidence for the relevant segment.
5. **Legal and privacy gate:** Review commercial terminology, guarantees, tracking, profiling, scarcity, automated communication, and case-study claims.
6. **Quotation gate:** Direct quotes require exact wording, primary URL or edition, author, date, and context.
7. **Release gate:** Research acceptance or file persistence does not authorize publication, deployment, or external reuse.

## Final recommendations

1. Quarantine the February file as historical input and remove all instructions that treat it as agent authority.
2. Retain customer-as-hero, guide-like empathy, clarity, specific proof, and a simple next step as optional editorial heuristics.
3. Delete the false neuroscience, deterministic psychology, invented ratios, and behavioral mind-reading.
4. Treat SB7 and PEACE as vendor drafting frameworks, clearly separated from peer-reviewed evidence and Obenan validation.
5. Replace fear, shame, status, urgency, and prescription with evidenced concern, credible relief, and buyer agency.
6. Use accurate commercial and legal terms instead of universal luxury euphemisms.
7. Gate every product claim through current capability evidence, approval state, destination limits, and downstream boundaries.
8. Rebuild the quotation bank from exact primary-source wording and remove all unverified attributions.
9. Do not promote any rule or copy into brand canon until Seven, Product, customer evidence, and Legal/privacy gates are satisfied.
