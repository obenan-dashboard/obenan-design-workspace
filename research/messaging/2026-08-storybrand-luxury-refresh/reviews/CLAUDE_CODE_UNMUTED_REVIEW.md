## 1. Executive verdict

The four proposed files are the best governance work this messaging programme has produced, and they are not yet messaging. Codex built a claims constitution: a complete rule set on hero and guide, platform neutrality, assumption-free recognition, a claim-state ladder from observed to verified, verb gates, and locale governance. Every one of those rules is right, and almost every one matches founder direction that was already settled in August. That part should be approved.

What the files do not yet do is make anyone feel anything. The synthesis is written in the voice of a compliance reviewer describing a warm peer, which is not the same as being one. The north star (`MESSAGING_CORE_v2.md:11`) is internal strategy language: no customer would ever say "carries the supported ongoing work while the customer stays informed and in control." The word "supported" appears as a hedge in nearly every illustrative line, and a hedge repeated becomes the message. The illustrative patterns at `MESSAGING_CORE_v2.md:112-115` are the weakest lines in the system, and they are the only lines a future agent will actually copy.

The research is strong exactly where it is skeptical and weak where it needs to commit. It correctly demolishes the February source's false neuroscience, fixed ratios, fear tactics and autonomy claims. It then stops short of the commercial judgment that would make the guidance useful: what the one sentence is, what the emotional payoff is, and which conditional verbs are in fact already true on the live product.

Disposition in one line: approve the rules, reject the voice, and send the synthesis back for one targeted revision that adds a customer-language layer, a product-proof pass against the live site, and the Unmuted and website-copy lessons the research never saw.

## 4. What remains too abstract, safe, mechanical, generic, instructional, or internally worded

- The north star is a specification, not a north star. `MESSAGING_CORE_v2.md:11` reads like an acceptance criterion. A north star must be repeatable by a salesperson under pressure.
- "Supported" is doing the work that confidence should do. It appears in the relief promise (`:21`), the language families (`:74-77`), and the transformation outputs (`APPLIED_MESSAGING_GUIDE_v2.md:149-150, 159-162, 174-175`). Customers do not hear "supported" as honesty; they hear it as small print. The honest boundary belongs in the proof layer, not in every sentence.
- The illustrative microcopy (`MESSAGING_CORE_v2.md:112-115`) is grey. "You do not have to follow every change alone" is the best of the four and it still sounds like a support-desk auto-reply. "See what Obenan can handle for you" is a feature tour invitation.
- The transformation table (`APPLIED_MESSAGING_GUIDE_v2.md:145-151`) is where the voice problem is most visible. "Let Obenan carry the supported routine work" and "Keep supported business information current as AI changes" would not survive five seconds on a homepage. The table proves the rule and disproves the voice.
- There is no ambition. Every line is defensive. The research is right that fear and shame are out (`06-painkiller-peer-voice.md:227`), but it then removes desire too. The settled founder direction in the handoff says "concern about keeping up with AI change"; the Voice of Customer report itself records that 76 percent of operators see technology as an edge (`05-voice-of-customer.md:100`). Relief without any upside reads as reassurance for a customer who was never excited. A peer holds your hand and also says "this is going to be good."
- The guide tells agents what not to do eleven ways (`APPLIED_MESSAGING_GUIDE_v2.md:237-251`) and shows what to do in almost none. Agents will draft toward the rejection list.
- Internal vocabulary leaks: "bounded relief", "supported ongoing work", "claim record", "autonomy overread", "route-specific". Correct internally, unusable in a brief handed to a copywriter.

## 7. Painkiller and human-language assessment

The research defines the painkiller correctly (`06-painkiller-peer-voice.md:107-117`): external change, bounded load, what Obenan carries, preserved control, returned proof. The synthesis then writes it in a voice no peer uses. Three specific failures:

1. Recognition is stated, not felt. "AI keeps changing" is true and inert. Recognition works when it names the moment, not the trend: someone is asking right now, and the question is whether you are the answer. That is still assumption-free; it is about the customer's customers, not the customer.
2. Relief is hedged at the point of delivery. "You do not have to follow every change alone" is close. "You do not have to keep up with this. We do." is the same promise with a spine, and it is still bounded because "this" is defined one line later by the actual work.
3. Control is written as governance, not as dignity. "See what changed and what still needs your decision" is a UI string. The human version is that the final say stays with the customer, and nothing else lands on their desk.

The files also lack an emotional arrival and departure. The best line in the whole programme for this is one the research never saw: the customer arrives thinking "we are already behind, I do not know what AI is saying about us," and should leave thinking "Obenan is watching this for us, it will tell us what matters, handle the work, and keep us ready." That pair should be in the Core as the acceptance test for any surface.

## 8. Agentic differentiation and claim-boundary assessment

The claim boundaries are right and the differentiation is absent. The Core forbids guarantees and unlimited autonomy (`:79-80`), correctly. It never states what makes Obenan different from another listings or review product, which the handoff names as the commercial risk.

Two corrections from verified evidence:

- Differentiation exists and is provable today: one intelligence across every location, working through the assistant the team already uses, with approval before anything goes live, and a demonstration on the live site. That is neither a listings tool nor a dashboard. The Core should say so in one sentence and point to the proof.
- The competitive context has changed since the research cutoff framing: "agentic" is now the headline word of nearly every competitor, so leading with the word would lose differentiation rather than create it. The Core's instinct to keep category language out of the lead is therefore commercially right for a second reason the files do not state.

Claim verbs: the live homepage already states, in first-party copy, that Obenan prepares posts and photos and waits for approval, drafts review replies in the owner's tone, answers customer questions from approved facts, and that replies go out on approval or under a configured auto-reply rule. Those are current public claim states. DR-04 should pre-clear that exact vocabulary rather than block it behind a register that does not yet exist. "Monitors", "continuous", "automatic" and "no additional dashboard" remain correctly conditional.

## 9. Information-architecture implications (copy-focused)

- Primary onboarding: the hero CTA must say what happens. `Start now` fails the Guide's own CTA rule (`:53`). The free audit was the locked dominant action in August; if `/get-started/` remains the primary route, its label must describe the connection step without naming a provider.
- ObiGEO and AI visibility entry: `/ai-presence/` and the external snapshot are two doors to one diagnostic. Copy cannot make sense of this until the 2026-08-09 IA decision ships; guidance should describe the intended end state and flag the interim.
- Demo: one quiet secondary path from the hero, labelled for the multi-location buyer.
- Free-value family: three live experiences plus the external audit. The family name is open; "Checkups" is the only candidate with any team exposure and has no comprehension evidence. Do not name it in the Core.
- Footer and header: the single "AI Visibility" item decision is unshipped; guidance must not assume it is live.

## 13. Disagreement register

- Ambition versus relief. The research treats any upward frame as adjacent to status pressure. My judgment is commercial, not factual: a multi-location buyer is paid for guests won, and a page that only soothes will be respected and not bought. No stronger factual evidence on Obenan segments exists either way; DR-02 testing would settle it.
- "Supported" as a word. The research uses it to stay truthful. I believe truthfulness belongs in the proof layer and the boundary statement, and that the word in every sentence reads as doubt. This is judgment about reader perception, consistent with 05:37's plain-vocabulary finding but not proven by it.
- "Capable colleague" customer-facing. The research proposes it as eligible posture. I would keep it internal; the phrase invites the anthropomorphism the Core itself rejects. Judgment call.
- DR-04 strictness. The research blocks all conditional verbs pending a register. I read the live public site as already establishing several claim states at their exact public scope. This is a factual disagreement resolvable by the product owner in one sitting.

## 14. Final recommendation

Targeted revision round, not another research round. The evidence base is sufficient and the rules are sound. What is missing is a voice layer and a product-proof pass, both of which take days, not weeks. After that round, the material is ready for founder decision on DR-01 through DR-06.
