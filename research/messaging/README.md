# Messaging research lane

This lane holds evidence-backed research used to improve Obenan messaging.
Research outputs are findings, not final brand decisions.

## Shared research contract

Every specialist must:

1. read the repository governance and the immutable source snapshot;
2. use current, directly accessible evidence and record retrieval dates;
3. prefer primary, official, and peer-reviewed sources;
4. separate observed evidence, source interpretation, inference, and proposal;
5. identify contradictions, weak evidence, and non-transferable ideas;
6. avoid named-platform anchoring in proposed customer language;
7. avoid assumptions about a prospect's team, hiring, skills, workload, tools,
   maturity, or current operating model;
8. preserve a warm peer-to-peer voice focused on felt concern and credible
   relief, not fear amplification or professor-like education;
9. avoid guaranteed discovery, recommendation, revenue, booking, ranking,
   indexing, or autonomous external execution claims;
10. cite sources with direct URLs, dates, and DOI or publication identifiers
    where available;
11. write only the file assigned by the orchestrator;
12. never commit, push, publish, deploy, message externally, or edit root
    governance.

## Coordination

Concurrent research may be read-only. Durable writes are serialized by the
orchestrator. A specialist writes only after receiving an explicit write token
for its assigned file.
