## Evidence states

| State | Meaning in this synthesis |
| --- | --- |
| Observed evidence | Direct content, measurement, or current first-party statement recorded by a cited source. It proves only what was observed within that source's scope and date. |
| Dataset claim | A statement made by the February Diamond V1 dataset. It is not accepted merely because it is preserved. |
| Inference | A bounded conclusion drawn from evidence, with transfer limits retained. |
| Proposal | A messaging rule or wording direction recommended for founder review. It is not approved canon. |

Framework and consultancy material establishes what its provider teaches. It is not causal proof. First-party product material establishes a public claim state. It is not independent delivery proof.

## Explicit evidence gaps

- No first-party interviews, call transcripts, or controlled message tests with current, former, or prospective Obenan customers were available.
- No reviewed evidence shows that the proposed messaging causes conversion, revenue, bookings, recommendation, retention, or trust calibration for Obenan.
- Voice-of-customer evidence is predominantly U.S. and English-language, with stronger multi-location than single-location material.
- The founder-directed AI-change tension is strategically authoritative but not proven universal or phrased in representative Obenan customer language.
- Current product code, tests, deployment identity, runtime behavior, authorization, receipts, and failure recovery were outside this synthesis workspace.
- Public product pages provide claim state, not independent production or destination-level proof.
- Preferred-assistant access, reduced dashboard dependence, and automatic or ongoing behavior lack a current support matrix in this evidence base.
- No native Obenan customer research or comprehension testing was available for the seven public locales.
- The Spanish market and address model remain unspecified; Portuguese Brazil and Portuguese Portugal require separate evidence and ownership.
- Legal and privacy analysis was not supplied for representation language, personalization, tracking, profiling, scarcity, automated outreach, or agentic authority.
- Several February quotations and Luxury Academy aphorisms lack retrievable primary provenance.

## Product-proof gates

| Gate | Minimum proof before a customer claim |
| --- | --- |
| Capability | Current jobs, objects, sources, systems, locales, cadence, coverage, and exclusions. |
| Authority | Owner, role and tenant binding, least privilege, approval policy, duration, revocation, and no privilege expansion. |
| State | Distinct observed, inferred, recommended, prepared, approved, delivered, verified, partial, failed, and unknown states. |
| Execution | Exact target, authorized action, provider or system receipt, audit record, idempotency or replay protection. |
| Verification | Authoritative read-back or downstream postcondition against stated success criteria. |
| Control | Tested correction, approval, pause, stop, override, reversal, rollback, and escalation at meaningful times. |
| Reliability | Coverage, health, error rates, false positives and negatives, timeouts, retries, exceptions, and monitoring over an appropriate period. |
| Security and privacy | Identity, tenant isolation, token handling, consent, purpose, retention, disclosure, audit, and incident recovery review. |
| Interface | End-to-end positive, negative, authorization, revocation, accessibility, locale, compatibility, outage, and recovery tests for each claimed route. |
| Customer evidence | Direct research showing the claimed benefit reduces burden without reducing comprehension, control, error detection, or confidence. |
| Release refresh | Named owner rechecks time-sensitive counts, workflows, terms, cases, and source URLs at release time. |

A proposal, prototype, draft, isolated test, receipt, route response, preview, deployment, or first-party page proves only its own rung. None alone establishes a production-wide customer claim.
