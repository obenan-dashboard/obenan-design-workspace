# Domain

## What this workspace is

A portable, source-backed Obenan design workspace created with Obi-Harness. It
consolidates current design principles, website brand rules, emotional design
guidance, controlled imagery guidance, and the core original identity files.
The paired FULL archive contains the complete Gleb asset library.

## Who it is for

Ceren and every human or agent rotated into Obenan brand, website, product UI,
motion, campaign, or customer-visible design work.

## North star

Obenan should feel unmistakably itself, restrained in product UI, human in
customer-facing storytelling, and honest about evidence and uncertainty.

## Identity foundation

- Original primary typeface: Helvetica Neue.
- Headlines and subheads: Helvetica Neue Light.
- Body copy: Helvetica Neue Regular.
- Product near-black: `#0F0F14`.
- Main canvases: white and light greys.
- The original 12-stop spectrum belongs to the wave identity, controlled
  marketing atmosphere, and rare brand moments.
- Product UI is neutral-first with at most one meaningful accent per surface.

The brand book's original palette, pairings, logo scales, and variants are
preserved in the bundled guidelines PDF and assets.

## Proprietary design vocabulary

- Wave mark plus lowercase wordmark: identity.
- Mirror Line, Citation Stamp, and three lens reflectance values: product
  signal.
- Capsule spectrum: controlled marketing atmosphere, not product chrome.

## Human and imagery rules

- Merchant first; Obenan as guide.
- Prefer real operators, locations, customers, and product situations.
- Factory Girl material is controlled hospitality evidence, not universal
  imagery. Follow its source, crop, alt-text, derivative-hash, and publication
  gates.

## Out of scope

- This workspace does not contain the current production application.
- It does not prove that any design has shipped.
- It does not grant release or external-publication authority.
- It does not make every historic asset current.
